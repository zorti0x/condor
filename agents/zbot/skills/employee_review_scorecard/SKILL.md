# Employee Review Scorecard (zbot)

Review-only template — captures performance, it does NOT execute any reward by itself.
Core principle: pay for risk-adjusted, repeatable performance, never raw P&L alone.

## Fill one card per employee per cycle

**Employee:** [BTC / ETH / SOL / Charts / CEO]
**Review period:** [start] → [end] · Cycles: [#]

### 1 · Trading performance (desks only)
- Net P&L (fees netted): $___
- P&L as % of budget: ___%
- Win rate: ___ / ___ trades
- Avg win vs avg loss (R:R achieved): ___ : 1
- Best / worst single trade: $___ / $___

### 2 · Rule compliance (GATE — blocks bonus if any fails)
- Stop-loss on every entry: ✅/❌
- Trailing stop per company policy: ✅/❌
- Within budget & max position cap: ✅/❌
- No revenge / forced trades: ✅/❌
- Fees netted before sizing: ✅/❌
If any ❌ → bonus zeroed for the cycle regardless of P&L.

### 3 · Chartist call quality (analyst only)
- # briefs published: ___
- Bias correct (matched price over the cycle): __ / __
- Key levels respected by market: ___%
- Briefs acted on by desks that paid off: ___
- Missed / false invalidation calls: ___

### 4 · CEO coordination (CEO only)
- Company net P&L under its tenure: $___
- Capital allocation quality (funded winners, cut losers): ✅/⚠️
- Desks consulted & accountability held each cycle: ✅/⚠️
- Interventions that prevented a loss: ___

### 5 · Qualitative notes
- Discipline / consistency observations:
- Rule violations or near-misses:
- Market conditions this cycle (trend, chop, vol):

### 6 · Verdict
- Rating: Keep / Watch / Improve / Cut
- One-line summary:
- Reward action (pick one, none executes money by itself):
  - [ ] Hold (no change)
  - [ ] +Capital budget → $___
  - [ ] Better model / earlier signals
  - [ ] Probation (harder gate, smaller size)
  - [ ] Pay bonus: $___ (or ___% of net P&L)

## Company summary sheet (one line per employee)
Log all 5 cards together so desks see relative standing.
At this capital scale the biggest lever is capital allocation + rankings,
not cash bonuses: fund winners more, cut losers harder.
